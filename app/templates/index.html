Start here

Every video you make is a project: one script, one look, and the finished MP4, kept together in their own folder. You make a new project for each video, and nothing is shared between them.

Your first project comes with a short example script already written, so you can press Generate straight away and watch a real video come out the other end. Once you have seen it work, delete the example and write your own.

The first run downloads a voice for each line, so give it a minute. After that, only the lines you change are made again.
